Privacy

Clear data practices.

This policy describes the data handled by the current beta version of Build Your Own FPV. It should be reviewed before enabling accounts, analytics, advertising or payments.

Information handled by the site

The builder can store your mission, budget and selected component IDs in your browser so your unfinished build remains available. When you use “Save build,” the server stores the build name, mission, budget, selected component IDs, analysis result and creation time.

Technical information

The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL and request time for security, reliability and debugging. This version does not include a third-party analytics SDK.

External resources

Component cards may display images hosted by third-party sites and contain links to manufacturer or retailer pages. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Product images are requested without sending the page URL as a referrer where browser support allows it.

Data sharing

Build data is not sold. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers required to operate the site, or disclosed when legally required.

Retention and deletion

Browser storage can be removed through your browser settings. Saved builds in this beta should be treated as non-sensitive public-link data. To request deletion, provide the saved build link or ID to the support address below.

Do not submit sensitive information

Do not use build names to submit passwords, payment information, government identifiers, precise home addresses or other sensitive personal information.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@buildyourownfpv.com.
